LOCATION:

The property is located at Knockbrack, Athenry, Co. Galway and is situated approximately 3 miles from the Medieval and Heritage town of Athenry and 2.5 miles from Monivea Village on the main Athenry / Monivea Road.

All local residential amenities and facilities are available in the nearby town of Athenry and the picturesque village of Monivea. Galway City is within approximately 17 miles from the property. Newcastle NS is just under 1.5 miles with it’s fine National School, church and playing pitch.

Athenry is a highly sought after commuter town of Galway City which is just 13 miles and is steeped in local history and is a famous Medieval, Heritage and Market town. Athenry has excellent road and rail services and avails of Motorway access from the M6 Galway-Dublin route and with easy access on to the M17/M18 route further out from the town. Athenry train station boasts an excellent service to Galway/ Dublin and Athlone etc aswell as the Galway / Limerick route.

DESCRIPTION:

The property comprises a beautifully presented spacious 4 bedroom extended bungalow residence of approximately 1,520 sq ft and stands on a highly attractive large lawned site with some outbuildings. The property has a front tarmac driveway which wraps around to the rear offering a spacious forecourt with a stylish raised deck area being a lovely feature looking down over the expansive rear garden with picturesque country views.

The house benefits from a major renovation and upgrade works in recent years including the addition of a lovely rear family room extension annexe off the kitchen / dining room with patio doors leading out to the feature raised decking area allowing for modern family living. The upgrade works include quality new windows and doors throughout, dual central heating, with a feature new stove to family room, new bathroom suites, new kitchen with feature granite worktop, flooring and the lovely rear raised deck capturing the evening sun and overlooking the rear garden. The property also benefits from pumped insulation to cavity walls and attic insulation.

The house is beautifully finished and is in ready to walk into condition and equally the gardens and outside are truly impressive providing for a superb family home in a highly convenient location.
